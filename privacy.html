Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what information UtilityPuzzles ("we", "our", "the site") collects and how it is used. The short version: this site does not require accounts, does not ask for personal information, and all puzzles are generated locally in your browser.

Information we collect

No personal information is required. You do not need to register, sign in, or provide an email address to use any tool on this site. Puzzle generation happens entirely in your browser — your puzzle data is never sent to our servers.

Like most websites, our hosting provider and advertising partners may automatically collect standard technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type and pages visited. This information is used in aggregate for security and to understand how the site is used.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

This site displays ads served by Google AdSense and other third-party advertising partners. These partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads on this site.
  • Google's use of the advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies by visiting aboutads.info.

For more information about how Google uses data when you use its partners' sites, please visit How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites.

Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. Advertising partners may set cookies to remember your preferences and show relevant ads. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but this may affect the functionality of some websites. Disabling cookies does not stop puzzle generation on this site.

Links to other sites

This site may contain links to other websites. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of third-party sites and encourage you to review their privacy policies.

Children's privacy

This site is a general-audience site and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact us at contact@helpuhelpurself.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent changes.
