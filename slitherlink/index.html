How to use the slitherlink maker

Pick a difficulty and a fresh puzzle appears instantly — every puzzle is different, and each one has exactly one solution.

  • Reading the grid: each number says exactly how many of that cell's four sides your loop must use. A 0 means none of its sides are used; a 3 means three of them.
  • Solving: draw one continuous closed loop. It can turn corners, but it can never cross itself, branch, or leave gaps. There is always exactly one way to draw it.
  • Draw on screen: click a grid line to draw it, click again to cross it out, click again to clear. Check highlights anything wrong. Right-click crosses a line out directly.
  • Printing: "Print / PDF" prints the blank puzzle — up to 2 per sheet. "Print Answers" prints the completed loop.

The rules of slitherlink

  1. Draw a single closed loop along the grid lines — it never crosses or branches.
  2. Every numbered cell must have exactly that many of its four sides used by the loop.
  3. Cells without a number impose no rule at all.
  4. The loop may touch itself at corners, but it must be one continuous, unbroken ring.
  5. There is exactly one solution, reachable by logic.

Frequently asked questions

What is a slitherlink puzzle?

Slitherlink is a logic puzzle by Nikoli (the Sudoku company). Draw a single closed loop along the grid lines so that every numbered cell has exactly that many of its four sides used by the loop. Cells without a number impose no rule at all. The loop must never cross or branch.

How do I draw the loop on screen?

Click any grid line between two dots to draw it, click again to cross it out as a note, and click a third time to clear it. Draw your loop, then press Check to see which lines are right. You can also right-click (or long-press) a line to cross it out directly.

Is the slitherlink puzzle generator free?

Yes. Every puzzle generator on this site is completely free and unlimited. There is no account, no sign-up and no watermark, and you can generate as many puzzles as you like.

How do I print a slitherlink puzzle or save it as a PDF?

Click "Print / PDF" to print the blank puzzle, or "Print Answers" for the completed loop. In the print dialog choose "Save as PDF" to download a file. You can print up to 2 puzzles per sheet.
