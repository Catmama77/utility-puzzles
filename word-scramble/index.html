How to use the word scramble maker

Choose a theme and difficulty, and a fresh worksheet appears instantly — every worksheet is different, and no two on a page are ever the same. Each row shows a word with its letters mixed up; type the correct word next to it. Turn on “Show clues” and each scramble comes with a one-line hint from our word database.

  • Difficulty: Easy uses short words (3–5 letters), Medium uses 4–7 letters, and Hard uses 5–9 letters.
  • Themes: Animals, Food & Drink, Sports, Countries, Jobs and Nature — the same categories as our other puzzles.
  • Fair scrambles: no word is left in its original order, and the generator avoids scrambling a word into a different real word.
  • Solve on screen: Type each answer, then click “Check” — wrong answers are highlighted in red until you fix them.
  • Printing: Click “Print / PDF” for a worksheet with blank answer lines, or “Print Answers” for the completed sheet. In the print dialog choose “Save as PDF” to download a file.

The rules of word scramble

  1. Each row shows the letters of a real word, mixed up.
  2. Rearrange the letters to form the correct word — every letter is used exactly once.
  3. If clues are turned on, the clue describes the answer word; use it when you're stuck.
  4. Every word on the sheet is different, and each appears only once.
  5. When you think you've solved a row, write or type the word in the answer line next to it.

Frequently asked questions

How do you solve a word scramble?

Rearrange the mixed-up letters to form a real word. Start by looking for common letter pairs, prefixes and suffixes, then try the vowels in different positions. If clues are turned on, the clue tells you what kind of thing the answer is.

Is the word scramble generator free?

Yes. Every generator on this site is completely free and unlimited. There is no account, no sign-up and no watermark, and you can generate as many worksheets as you like.

How do I print a word scramble worksheet or save it as a PDF?

Click “Print / PDF” for the worksheet with blank answer lines, or “Print Answers” for the completed sheet with every word unscrambled. In the print dialog choose “Save as PDF” to download a file.

Can I use these worksheets in my classroom?

Yes. Worksheets are generated by original software, so you are free to print and use them in classrooms and at home. Turn on clues to make a vocabulary exercise, or leave them off for pure spelling practice.
