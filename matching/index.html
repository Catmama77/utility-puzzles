How to use the matching maker

Pick a theme, a worksheet type and a size, and a fresh worksheet appears instantly — every one is different, and no two worksheets on a page repeat the same word list.

  • Matching worksheet: words are numbered 1, 2, 3… on the left; the clues are lettered A, B, C… in a shuffled order on the right. Draw lines (or click a word then its clue on screen) to pair them.
  • Flashcards: each card shows a word with its clue underneath — print, cut out, and use them for self-testing or group games.
  • Sizes: Easy gives 8 pairs, Medium 12 and Hard 16 — useful for differentiating in class.
  • Printing: “Print / PDF” prints the blank worksheet — up to 2 per sheet. “Print Answers” prints it with every pair already matched.

The rules of matching

  1. Every numbered word matches exactly one lettered clue, and every clue matches exactly one word.
  2. Draw a line from each word to its clue — or, on screen, click a word and then its matching clue.
  3. There are no unused clues and no unused words: the numbers and letters always line up.
  4. When every pair is matched, check the answers to confirm you were right.

Frequently asked questions

How do you solve a matching worksheet?

Each word in the numbered column matches exactly one clue in the lettered column. Draw a line from each word to its clue, or click a word and then a clue on screen — correct pairs are marked.

Is the matching worksheet maker free?

Yes. Every generator on this site is completely free and unlimited. There is no account, no sign-up and no watermark, and you can generate as many worksheets as you like.

Can I print flashcards?

Yes. Switch the mode to Flashcards and print — each card shows a word on top and its clue underneath, ready to cut out. Choose 8, 12 or 16 cards per worksheet.

How do I print the matching worksheet with answers?

Click “Show Answers” to mark every correct pair on screen, or “Print Answers” to print the worksheet with the pairs already matched.
