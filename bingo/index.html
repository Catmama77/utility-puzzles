How to use the bingo card maker

Pick a card type, grid size and how many cards you want, and a fresh set of cards appears instantly — every card is different. Classic number bingo uses the traditional B-I-N-G-O columns: B holds 1–15, I holds 16–30, N holds 31–45, G holds 46–60 and O holds 61–75. Word bingo works the same way, but with words from a theme instead of numbers.

  • Grid sizes: 3 × 3 for a fast game or young kids, 4 × 4 as a middle step, and the classic 5 × 5 with a free center square.
  • Word bingo: Choose from the same categories as our other puzzles — Animals, Food & Drink, Sports, Countries, Jobs and Nature. Great for vocabulary, sight words and spelling practice.
  • Cards per page: Print 1, 2, 3 or 4 cards on each 8½ × 11 sheet. Bigger batches (up to 100 cards) automatically flow onto extra pages, 1–4 cards per page as you choose.
  • Every card is different: the generator checks each new card against the rest of the batch and re-rolls until no two cards match — generate as many variations as you need.
  • Call sheet: The caller's list is shown under the cards and prints on its own page. Click items on screen to tick them off as you draw.
  • Printing: Click “Print Cards / PDF” for the cards, or “Print Call Sheet” for the caller's list. In the print dialog choose “Save as PDF” to download a file.

The rules of bingo

  1. Each player gets a card with a grid of numbers (or words). No two cards are alike.
  2. The caller draws numbers or words one at a time — use the call sheet to keep track and announce each one clearly.
  3. Players mark every called number or word that appears on their card.
  4. The first player to complete a line — a full row, column or diagonal — shouts “Bingo!” and wins. The host decides before the game whether a single line or a full card wins.
  5. If a card has a free center square, it counts as already marked from the start.
  6. When someone calls “Bingo!”, the host checks their card against the drawn numbers on the call sheet before declaring the win.

Frequently asked questions

What is bingo?

Bingo is a game where players mark numbers (or words) on their cards as the caller draws them. The first player to complete a row, column or diagonal — or the whole card — shouts “Bingo!” and wins.
