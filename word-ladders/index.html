Word Ladder Maker

Word ladders are one of the classic classroom puzzles: get from the start word to the end word by changing exactly one letter at a time, keeping every rung a real word. Choose a difficulty, and a fresh ladder appears instantly — solve on screen or print. Great for spelling practice, phonics work and early-finisher time.

How to use the word ladder maker

Pick a difficulty and a fresh ladder appears instantly — no two ladders on a page are ever the same. The start and end words are given; the rungs between them are yours to fill.

  • Solving: work from the start word and change one letter at a time, checking that each new rung is a real word. Working backward from the end word helps when you're stuck.
  • Difficulties: Easy needs about 2 steps, Medium about 3, Hard about 4 or more — the ladders draw from a large word bank so there's always a valid route.
  • Check: click Check and any rung that isn't the intended word is highlighted — the status line tells you when every rung is correct.
  • Printing: “Print / PDF” prints the ladder with blank rungs — up to 2 per sheet. “Print Answers” prints the completed ladder.

The rules of word ladders

  1. Every rung must be a real English word.
  2. Each step changes exactly one letter — never add, remove or rearrange letters.
  3. Keep the same word length all the way from start to end.
  4. Use the fewest steps you can; every ladder here has a solution.

Frequently asked questions

What is a word ladder?

A word ladder is a chain of words where every step differs from the previous word by exactly one letter — for example CAT → RAT → RAM. You are given the start and end words and must fill in the rungs between them.

Is the word ladder maker free?

Yes. Every generator on this site is completely free and unlimited. There is no account, no sign-up and no watermark, and you can generate as many ladders as you like.

How do I print a word ladder?

Click “Print / PDF” to print the puzzle with blank rungs, or “Print Answers” for the completed ladder. In the print dialog choose “Save as PDF” to download a file.

What do the difficulty levels mean?

Easy ladders need about 2 steps, Medium about 3 and Hard about 4 or more. Longer ladders give more space to explore different routes.
