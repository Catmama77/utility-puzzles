How to use the word search maker

Choose a theme, grid size and difficulty, and a fresh word search appears instantly — every puzzle is different, and no two searches on a page are ever the same. The word list is grouped by word length so younger solvers can start with the short words.

  • Difficulty: Easy hides words across and down (forward only). Medium adds backward words. Hard adds diagonal words in every direction — the real challenge.
  • Grid sizes: 8 × 8 for a quick warm-up or young kids, 10 × 10 as the everyday size, and 12 × 12 for a longer hunt.
  • Themes: Animals, Food & Drink, Sports, Countries, Jobs and Nature — the same categories as our other puzzles.
  • Solve on screen: Click the first letter of a word you've spotted, then click its last letter. If it's a word on the list, it's marked found and struck off the list.
  • Every search is different: the generator re-rolls until no two puzzles in a batch share the same word placements.
  • Printing: Click “Print / PDF” for the blank puzzle, or “Print Answers” for the completed grid with every word highlighted. In the print dialog choose “Save as PDF” to download a file.

The rules of word search

  1. Every word on the list is hidden somewhere in the grid, spelled with consecutive letters.
  2. Words may run across, down or diagonally — and, depending on the difficulty, backward as well as forward.
  3. Words never turn corners: each one follows a single straight line.
  4. Letters in the grid can be shared by more than one word — that's normal, not an error.
  5. Every word on the list appears exactly once; there are no duplicates to find.
  6. When you think you've found a word, mark it — circle it on paper, or click its first and last letter on screen.

Frequently asked questions

How do I solve a word search?

Look for each word on the list hidden in the grid of letters. Words can run forward or backward, across, down or diagonally depending on the difficulty level. When you spot one, mark it — click the first and last letter on screen to mark it found, or circle it on a printed puzzle.

Is the word search generator free?

Yes. Every generator on this site is completely free and unlimited. There is no account, no sign-up and no watermark, and you can generate as many puzzles as you like.

How do I print a word search or save it as a PDF?

Click “Print / PDF” to print the blank puzzle, or “Print Answers” for the completed grid with every word highlighted. In the print dialog choose “Save as PDF” to download a file you can share or reprint.

Can I use these word searches in my classroom?

Yes. Puzzles are generated by original software, so you are free to print and use them in classrooms, clubs and at home. The themed word lists make great vocabulary and spelling practice.
