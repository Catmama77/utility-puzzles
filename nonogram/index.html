Nonogram Maker

Reveal the hidden picture! Every nonogram (also called picross or griddler) is a logic puzzle: the numbers at the start of each row and column tell you how many runs of filled squares belong there. Compare the clues, fill in the squares, and the picture appears. Every puzzle has exactly one solution — and every sheet is unique. Choose a size and difficulty, solve on screen or print.

How to use the nonogram maker

Pick a difficulty and a fresh nonogram appears instantly — every sheet is different, and no two sheets on a page repeat the same puzzle.

  • How it works: the numbers at the start of each row and column list the runs of filled squares in that line, in order. A clue of “2 1” means a block of 2 filled squares, then at least one empty square, then a block of 1.
  • Solve on screen: click a square to fill it black, right-click or Shift+click to mark a square × (definitely empty). Use the Fill / Mark × buttons for touch screens, and the arrow keys to move around.
  • Strategy: start with rows or columns whose clue numbers add up to nearly the full length — those force most of their squares. A clue of “8” on a 10-wide row fills 6 of its 10 squares in every solution.
  • Printing: “Print / PDF” prints the blank puzzle — up to 2 per page. “Print Answers” prints the completed grid.

The rules of nonograms

  1. Each row and column has a list of clue numbers, read from the edge toward the grid.
  2. Each clue number is the length of one block of filled squares in that line.
  3. Blocks are separated by at least one empty square, and the clues appear in order.
  4. Every puzzle has exactly one solution, reachable by logic alone.

Frequently asked questions

How do you solve a nonogram?

The numbers at the start of each row and column tell you how many runs of filled squares that line contains, in order. A clue of “2 1” means a block of 2 filled squares, then a gap, then 1 filled square. Compare the row and column clues to work out which squares must be filled and which must stay empty.

Is the nonogram maker free?

Yes. Every generator on this site is completely free and unlimited. There is no account, no sign-up and no watermark, and you can generate as many puzzles as you like.

What do the difficulty levels change?

Easy nonograms are 8×8 grids, Medium are 10×10, and Hard are 12×12. Bigger grids have longer clue lists and more combinations, so they take more logic to solve. Every puzzle has exactly one solution.

How do I print a nonogram?

Click “Print / PDF” to print the blank puzzle, or “Print Answers” for the completed grid. In the print dialog choose “Save as PDF” to download a file.
