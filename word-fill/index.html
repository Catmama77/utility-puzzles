How to use the word fill-in maker

A word fill-in (also called a word fit or fill-it-in puzzle) works like a crossword without clues: the grid shows slots of different lengths, and your job is to fit the listed words into the right slots. The word list groups words by length, and each word shows the number of its slot. Overlapping letters give you hints as you go.

  • Difficulty: Easy uses short words on a small grid. Medium mixes short and mid-length words. Hard uses longer words on a bigger, denser grid.
  • Printing: Click “Print / PDF” for a clean puzzle page, or “Print Answers” for the completed grid. In the print dialog choose “Save as PDF” to download a file.
  • Categories: Animals, Food & Drink, Sports, Countries, Jobs and Nature. Every puzzle is generated fresh, so you can make unlimited puzzles.
