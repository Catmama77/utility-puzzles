How to use the word fill-in maker

A word fill-in (also called a word fit or fill-it-in puzzle) works like a crossword without clues: the grid shows slots of different lengths, and your job is to fit the listed words into the right slots. The word list groups words by length, and each word shows the number of its slot. Overlapping letters give you hints as you go.

  • Difficulty: Easy uses short words on a small grid. Medium mixes short and mid-length words. Hard uses longer words on a bigger, denser grid.
  • Solving: Type letters straight into the grid and use “Check” to highlight wrong entries in red — or print a blank puzzle to solve on paper.
  • Printing: Click “Print / PDF” for a clean puzzle page, or “Print Answers” for the completed grid. In the print dialog choose “Save as PDF” to download a file.
  • Categories: Animals, Food & Drink, Sports, Countries, Jobs and Nature. Every puzzle is generated fresh, so you can make unlimited puzzles.

Frequently asked questions

What is a word fill-in puzzle?

A word fill-in (also called a word fit or fill-it-in puzzle) is a crossword-style grid with numbered slots and a word list grouped by length. You fit the listed words into the matching slots using the overlapping letters as hints.

Is the word fill-in puzzle maker free?

Yes. Every puzzle generator on this site is completely free and unlimited. There is no account, no sign-up and no watermark, and you can generate as many puzzles as you like.

How do I print a puzzle or save it as a PDF?

Click the Print / PDF button (or Print Answers for the completed grid). In the print dialog choose “Save as PDF” to download a file you can share or reprint.

Can I use the generated puzzles in my classroom?

Yes. All puzzles are generated by original software with original content, so you are free to print and use them in classrooms, at events or at home.

Can I solve the word fill-in puzzle on screen?

Yes. Type letters directly into the grid and use the “Check” button to highlight wrong entries in red. You can also print a blank puzzle to solve on paper.
