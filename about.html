About this site

UtilityPuzzles is a collection of free, printable puzzle generators.

What we do

We build simple, useful tools that create puzzles on demand. Every puzzle is generated fresh by our own software — no puzzle books, no repeating content, no watermarks. You can generate as many puzzles as you like, print them, or save them as PDF files.

Our tools are designed for teachers, parents, activity organizers and anyone who enjoys pencil-and-paper puzzles. Each tool offers different difficulty levels and printable answer keys.

Our tools

  • Word Fill-In — word fit puzzles in six categories at three difficulty levels. Try it.
  • Crossword Maker — automatic crossword grids with clues, at three difficulty levels. Try it.
  • Number Fill-In — number fit puzzles with five themes and fresh random numbers every time. Try it.
