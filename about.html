About this site

Brainy Puzzles is a collection of free printable puzzle games for all ages and stages — play online, or download and print from any device.

Advertisement

What we do

We build simple tools that create puzzles on demand — every game is free to play online and free to download for printing. Every puzzle is generated fresh by our own software — no puzzle books, no repeating content, no watermarks.

Our games are designed for teachers, parents, activity organizers and anyone who enjoys pencil-and-paper puzzles. Each game offers different difficulty levels and printable answer keys.

Our tools

  • Word Fill-In — word fit puzzles in six categories at three difficulty levels. Try it.
  • Crossword Maker — automatic crossword grids with clues, at three difficulty levels. Try it.
  • Number Fill-In — number fit puzzles with five themes and fresh random numbers every time. Try it.
