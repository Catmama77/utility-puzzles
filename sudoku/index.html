How to use the sudoku maker

Every sudoku is a classic logic grid: fill each empty square so that every row, column and box contains the numbers 1 to 9 exactly once (1 to 6 on the junior grid, 1 to 4 on the mini grid). The numbers already in the grid are your starting clues — use elimination to work out what fits where.

  • Grid sizes: 4 × 4 Mini for young kids, 6 × 6 Junior as a first step up, and the classic 9 × 9 for everyone else.
  • Difficulty: Easy leaves more clues, Hard leaves fewer. Each puzzle is generated with exactly one solution — no guessing required, ever.
  • Solving: Click any empty square and type a number. Use “Check” to highlight wrong entries in red, or “Show Answers” to see the whole grid.
  • Printing: Pick 1, 2, 4, 6 or 8 puzzles per page — great for a whole class or a week of practice — then click “Print / PDF” for the blank puzzles or “Print Answers” for the completed grids. In the print dialog choose “Save as PDF” to download a file.

The rules of sudoku

  1. The grid is divided into boxes — nine 3×3 boxes on the classic 9×9 grid, four 2×2 boxes on the mini 4×4, and six 2×3 boxes on the junior 6×6.
  2. Some cells are already filled in. These are your clues — they are always correct and never change.
  3. Fill every empty cell so that each row, each column and each box contains every number exactly once: 1–9 on the 9×9, 1–6 on the 6×6, and 1–4 on the 4×4.
  4. No number may appear twice in any row, column or box.
  5. Every puzzle on this site has exactly one solution and can be solved by logic alone — no guessing is ever needed.
  6. A good starting move: pick a box or row that is nearly full, work out which numbers are missing, and use the rows and columns that cross it to eliminate options.

Frequently asked questions

What is a sudoku puzzle?

Sudoku is a logic puzzle played on a grid of squares divided into boxes. The goal is to fill every empty square so that each row, each column and each box contains the numbers 1 to 9 exactly once. No math is needed — just logic and elimination.

Is the sudoku puzzle generator free?

Yes. Every puzzle generator on this site is completely free and unlimited. There is no account, no sign-up and no watermark, and you can generate as many puzzles as you like.

How do I print a sudoku puzzle or save it as a PDF?

Click the Print / PDF button (or Print Answers for the completed grid). In the print dialog choose “Save as PDF” to download a file you can share or reprint.

Can I solve the sudoku on screen?

Yes. Empty squares are clickable — type a number directly into the grid. The Check button highlights any wrong entries in red, and you can solve on screen or print a blank puzzle to solve on paper.
