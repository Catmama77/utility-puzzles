Number Search Puzzle Maker

Number searches are the word search's number-loving cousin: a grid of digits with numbers hidden inside it, running in straight lines. Pick a size and difficulty, and a fresh puzzle appears instantly — every one is different. Great for math warm-ups, number recognition practice and anyone who prefers digits to letters.

How to use the number search maker

Choose a grid size and difficulty, and a fresh number search appears instantly — every puzzle is different, and no two puzzles on a page share the same number placements. The number list is grouped by digit count so you can start with the shortest numbers.

  • Difficulty: Easy hides two-digit numbers across and down. Medium adds three-digit numbers running in all four straight directions. Hard uses three- and four-digit numbers in every direction, including diagonals.
  • Grid sizes: 8 × 8 for a quick warm-up or young kids, 10 × 10 as the everyday size, 12 × 12 for a longer hunt.
  • Solve on screen: click the first digit of a number you've spotted, then its last digit. If it's on the list, it's marked found.
  • Printing: “Print / PDF” prints the blank grid — up to 2 per sheet. “Print Answers” prints the grid with every number highlighted.

The rules of number search

  1. Every number on the list appears exactly once in the grid, spelled with consecutive digits.
  2. Numbers run in straight lines — across, down or, depending on the difficulty, backward and diagonally.
  3. Numbers never turn corners, and they never overlap another number on the list.
  4. Digits in the grid can be shared between a listed number and the random filler digits around it — that's part of the challenge.
  5. When you find a number, mark it — circle it on paper, or click its first and last digit on screen.

Frequently asked questions

How do you solve a number search puzzle?

Each number on the list is hidden somewhere in the grid as consecutive digits running in a straight line. Find it and mark it — click the first digit and last digit on screen, or circle it on a printed puzzle.

Is the number search maker free?

Yes. Every generator on this site is completely free and unlimited. There is no account, no sign-up and no watermark, and you can generate as many puzzles as you like.

What do the difficulty levels mean?

Easy hides two-digit numbers running across and down. Medium hides two- and three-digit numbers running in all four straight directions. Hard hides three- and four-digit numbers in every direction, including diagonals.

How do I print a number search?

Click “Print / PDF” for the blank grid, or “Print Answers” for the grid with every number highlighted. In the print dialog choose “Save as PDF” to download a file.
